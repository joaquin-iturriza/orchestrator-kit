- Pillars: `CLAUDE.md`, `~/work/CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/context/**`, `plan/*prompt*.txt` and `.claude/agents/**` ->
  claudemd-keeper (which also reads the hooks' refusal texts); source -> repo-reviewer (`*.py`, `*.sh`, `tools`,
  `tests`, `scripts`, `.claude/settings.json`). Not counted: plan bookkeeping (`plan/**` but its prompt templates), data
  files (`*.json`, `*.log`, `*.out`, `*.err`), `runs/`, `sweeps/`. The table is PILLARS in
  `.claude/hooks/review_backlog.sh`.
- A backlog is everything since its pillar's watermark. Thresholds: any change for the CLAUDE.md pillar; 520 changed
  lines for code. Past its threshold, edits to the pillar's files and commits carrying its uncommitted changes are
  refused until its reviewer runs.
- Whatever the threshold, a merge into `trunk` and `plan done` need the commits merged or closed to sit inside a
  passed backlog.
- One reviewer per backlog: a launch while that pillar's reviewer holds its lock is refused and names the agent to
  continue with SendMessage. A blocking review keeps its lock so its fixes can be applied; the re-check continues the
  same reviewer, naming the fix commits. A lock expires after 180 min.
- `review_backlog.sh status` lists backlogs; `begin <name>` and `advance <name>` are the reviewer's own (the user's
  override is a `!` command). Watermarks: `.claude/.review_state/`.
- The reviewers (claudemd-keeper, repo-reviewer, design-reviewer) inherit the main session's model.
